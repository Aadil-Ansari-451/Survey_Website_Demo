About

Built on expertise and integrity

NB Onoya Geomatics and Construction Surveying Ltd. is a Vancouver-based survey firm serving construction, development, and infrastructure clients across British Columbia.

Our approach

Evidence-based methodology, field-tested discipline

Since inception, the organization has prioritized evidence-based methodologies that promote structural resilience and operational efficiency. We treat every control point as a commitment: checked, documented, and traceable.

Our crews combine seasoned field judgment with current GNSS, robotic, and aerial workflows — so the data we hand over is something engineers, contractors, and developers can build on without second-guessing.
